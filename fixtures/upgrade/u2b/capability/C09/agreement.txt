Authored fictional services agreement: External billing manual.
No real customer data or real-world legal advice.

Section 1 - Services and fees
The Customer, Fictional Cedar External billing manual Cooperative, engages the fictional Supplier to provide sensor reporting for one initial year. The Supplier will maintain a monthly work log and deliver a quarterly progress note. The service fee is 240 fictional units per month; milestone dates are planning estimates and do not alter exit or billing rights.

Section 2 - Renewal
After the initial year, a further service period exists only if both parties expressly consent in a signed writing.

Section 3 - Termination
The Customer can leave this arrangement without stating a cause by delivering written notice thirty calendar days before exit.

Section 4 - Payment
The billing manual maintained elsewhere determines when undisputed invoices received by the Customer fall due; no copy accompanies this agreement.

Section 5 - Confidentiality
Each party limits access to the other's fictional operational records to staff performing the services. Public catalogue descriptions are excluded. Return of a sample record does not discharge an unpaid invoice or change the notice procedure.

Section 6 - Delivery and responsibility
The Supplier supplies a monthly export and corrects a missing row within seven working days. The Customer designates a contact for service questions. No service-level remedy changes the separate convenience or renewal terms.

Section 7 - Definitions and reporting
A reporting month is the calendar month used in the work log. The log's closing date is not a contractual invoice trigger.

Section 8 - Records and entire arrangement
The parties keep their own copies of this fictional text. Commercial reminders and work-log retention periods do not amend the clauses above. A change in operational contact does not establish amendment priority. This example is synthetic and carries no real party obligation.
