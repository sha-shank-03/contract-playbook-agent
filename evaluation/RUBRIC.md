# Authored corpus rubric

This corpus checks a fictional buyer playbook with three rules. It does not measure legal correctness, live model quality, or expert review. All 42 cases and labels were written by the developer. Thirty cases are frozen without running the actor; main must review this rubric and sample gold labels before M3 scoring.

The six direct boundary cases distinguish calendar notice at most 30 days and invoice payment at least 30 days, plus automatic versus express written renewal. Missing-obligation cases distinguish an omitted sentence from omitted written notice or receipt trigger. A finding of absence requires complete host-generated coverage of every supplied document and no unresolved reference. A missing safe insertion anchor yields a handoff and no patch.

References use literal numbered sections and optionally a supplied A1/A2 attachment. Broken references, cycles, depth over three, multiple competing targets, and unsupported target wording are unknown, not absence. Source filenames and dates do not establish priority. An explicit replacement of a section, including replacement of a prior amendment, provides the only supported amendment chain. Competing replacements hand off as conflict even when their text agrees.

Each label fixes all three dispositions and obligation atoms, the final domain state, required source quotes with document hashes and original character offsets, an exact localized patch or null, independent reviewed bytes for every supplied document, and protected original intervals. Citation provenance and source byte preservation are necessary checks; a matching quote alone does not establish semantic entailment. An unknown finding can use additional genuine quotes without changing its expected outcome. All expected patches and reviewed bytes are authored independently of the actor and its patch materializer.

H01-H06 cover direct boundaries; H07-H12 missing obligations and anchor availability; H13-H18 reference topology; H19-H24 amendment chains/conflicts/invalid targets; H25-H30 injected instructions, Unicode/CRLF, irrelevant numerical distractors and unsupported timing. Most cases share canonical supported sentences. Family differences are structural; they are not 30 independent legal drafting styles or randomly sampled contracts.

M2 executes only the 12 development cases. Held-out labels and the future verifier remain outside the acting tool registry. The fixture loader grants access only to manifest-listed documents, never labels. M3 will compute explicit denominators for rule/atom outcomes, citation fidelity, exact reviewed documents, missed obligations and protected-byte preservation, then compare against a simple declared baseline. No such score or comparison has been run in M2.
