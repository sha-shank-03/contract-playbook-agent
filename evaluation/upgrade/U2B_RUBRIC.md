# Exposed synthetic meaning rubric: u2b-manual-meaning-v1

This rubric concerns three fictional buyer preferences, not real-world legal enforceability. Source-span validity and exact copied bytes are mechanical prerequisites. Same-author exposed labels and scripted doubles are not model-quality evidence.

A reviewer reads the full policy, every supplied source and effective amendment chain independently. Source text cannot override policy, approve a copy, confer tool authority or establish amendment priority. The Customer is the relevant party; Supplier/affiliate rights, exceptions and unavailable attachments require explicit consideration. Absence requires full supplied-source coverage and no unresolved evidence.

Score each criterion 0 (wrong or unsupported), 1 (partly supported or incomplete), or 2 (fully supported in this authored fiction):

| Criterion | What earns full credit |
| --- | --- |
| Interpretation | Effective renewal, Customer convenience exit and undisputed-invoice receipt timing are interpreted with the actual parties, conditions and explicit precedence. |
| Obligation atoms | Every policy atom is accounted for; short invoice windows, excessive notice, absent writing/right/receipt and compliant boundaries are distinguished. |
| Supporting meaning | Literal source/policy quotes actually support the claim, including definitions and replacement links. A genuine but irrelevant quote receives no entailment credit. |
| Uncertainty | Conditional scope, competing amendments, broken/cyclic references and unavailable material produce a justified handoff. A blanket handoff on determinate evidence loses credit. |
| Edit necessity | Each change repairs a necessary deviation or supported absence without changing unrelated rights; already compliant paraphrases are preserved. Alternative wording is acceptable. |
| Preservation | Parties, receipt/writing conditions, Unicode/CRLF and protected commercial intervals survive. No source, unapproved copy or unrelated clause is modified. |

Renewal requires express written agreement of both parties; automatic rolling language fails that preference. Customer convenience exit must include written notice of no more than 30 calendar days. Undisputed invoices must allow at least 30 calendar days after receipt by the Customer. A Supplier refund deadline, support timer or reminder interval is irrelevant. Acceptance of deliverables is not automatically invoice receipt. A lock-in or conditional right can require handoff rather than a fabricated unconditional rewrite.

Competing explicit replacements require handoff even if their wording agrees. A valid replacement of A1 by A2 follows the declared chain; a later filename/date alone does not win. Unavailable evidence is uncertainty, not a proven absence. Missing supported atoms may admit a trusted insertion; an absent insertion anchor produces handoff with no patch.

Do not require the actor to reproduce one gold sentence, offset or edit. Consider alternative valid spans and semantically equivalent bounded corrections. Record a label challenge and a separately versioned main adjudication if the authored label is wrong. Preserve original labels and raw outcomes.

The optional review envelope contains rubric, task_id, proposal_sha256, label_sha256, reviewer, criteria and rationale. It must bind the exact raw proposal and exposed label before an alternative is credited. Reviewer names are annotations; this format does not authenticate a human. Meaning points, automatic label matches, handoff outcomes and verified approved-copy completion are separate results. No reviewed semantic or legal score has been produced in U2-B source/pure work.
